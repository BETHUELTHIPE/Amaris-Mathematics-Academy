# School booking grade and chapter guides

CAPS and IEB Mathematics expose Grades 7–12 in the live-class search, video request and tutor availability create/edit forms. Mathematical Literacy exposes Grades 10–12. Student registration also includes Grade 7. A single selector and catalogue provide consistent dependent selections; changing programme, subject or grade clears the previous chapter.

The guide groups the major syllabus topics by grade. It is Amaris wording, not a reproduction of an official syllabus or a textbook's chapter numbers. IEB NSC uses CAPS as its base with its own assessment guidelines. Junior IEB-school support uses the CAPS-aligned guide and permits a learner's own school chapter or instructions. A custom chapter/assignment/exam option and the existing supporting documents allow school-specific scope. No IEB proprietary assessment document is bundled.

The server continues to receive the existing `programme`, `subject`, `level` and `topic` fields. Explicit custom entries are resolved before forwarding; payment amounts, permissions and tutor ownership remain server-controlled. Published availability is still required to book a live class. The feature creates no courses, tutor slots, payments or database migrations.

TVET and university retain their standard levels and a custom academic/module entry. Live search has a GET update button and native no-JavaScript text alternatives so server-rendered chapter choices can be refreshed before searching.
